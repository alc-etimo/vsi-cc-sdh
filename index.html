Case study 03 · Editorial judgement

Preserve voice.
Don’t normalize by default.

Dialect markers, register and character voice can carry meaning. The right choice depends on the intention, intelligibility, and narrative relevance, as well as the client's style guide.

03 / Language variation

Consistency is not the same as standardization.

For Deaf and Hard of Hearing viewers, the subtitle may be the only access to a speaker’s regional or social language cues. Removing every non-standard form can erase characterization.

At the same time, spelling variation should not be introduced just to imitate an accent. The decision needs to be deliberate and consistent.
